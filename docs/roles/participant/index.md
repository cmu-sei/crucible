# Participant Guide

!!! warning

     Always check the help documentation within your Crucible environment. Instructors and range builders often highly customize exercises and user interfaces. The guidance below provides general expectations but may not reflect your specific configuration. Typically, participant-facing documentation is integrated directly into the applications used. Refer to "in-app" help for environment-specific instructions and resources.

## Overview

Participants engage directly with the Crucible training environment to practice, demonstrate, and refine a broad range of cyber skills. This role maps to **Player** in the Player application and **Competitor** in the Gameboard application.

## What to Expect

You will typically:

- Join exercises through Player or Gameboard or a learning portal.
- Access virtual machines, networks, or scenarios provisioned for your team.
- Follow instructions from *your* instructor.
- Submit answers, artifacts, or defensive actions as required.
- Read incident updates on the [Gallery Wall](../../gallery/index.md#gallery-wall) and in the [Gallery Archive](../../gallery/index.md#gallery-archive).
- Discuss the incident with your team and record your team's assessment on the [CITE Scoresheet](../../cite/index.md#cite-scoresheet) each move.
- Receive feedback or scoring through the exercise interface.

## Quick Reference

| Issue | Try This First | Then |
| ------- | ---------------- | ------ |
| Platform won't load | Refresh or try another browser | Check for maintenance notices |
| VM or tool not responding | Restart or reconnect | Contact your instructor |
| Disconnected during event | Log back in via portal | Notify Exercise Control if issues persist |
| Scoring seems off | Refresh Gameboard | Report discrepancies to instructor |
| Can't change a CITE answer | Make sure the Scoresheet shows the current move | Ask your instructor whether your CITE team role lets you answer |

## Assessing Incidents in CITE

CITE asks your team to judge how severe the incident is at each move. It doesn't grade you. Depending on how your instructor set up the scoring model, the Scoresheet can show your team's answer for earlier moves next to the group average and the official score so you can discuss the differences.

- Answer the categories on the CITE Scoresheet as a team. If the Scoresheet has a **Submit** button, a member with the **Submitter** or **Owner** team role submits the team's answer.
- Use the arrows next to the move number to review earlier moves. You can edit only the current move.
- Teams see the official score for earlier moves. A person with the **Official Scorer** team role, not a participant, enters it.
- Review the **Actions** and **Duties** on the CITE Dashboard to see what your team should consider during the move.

## Learning Best Practices

- **Engage actively:** Treat each inject or task as a real incident.
- **Collaborate:** Coordinate with your teammates; range success is team success.
- **Keep notes:** Document commands, discoveries, and fixes for your after-action review.
- **Ask questions:** Clarify objectives early—ambiguity can be part of the challenge, but confusion isn't.

## Support

If you encounter critical issues:

1. Follow local escalation procedures first (as posted by your instructor).
2. Capture screenshots or logs when reporting issues.
3. Avoid changing or rebooting infrastructure unless directed.
